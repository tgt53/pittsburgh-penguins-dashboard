Player profiles

Season stats are grouped by team and can be switched between regular season and playoffs. They include standard NHL totals such as games, goals, assists, points, shots, and time on ice. Career totals follow the selected season type.

Skater profiles also include MoneyPuck on/off expected-goal data when a season row is available. Goalies do not have these skater measures.

The Download data button provides a Penguins regular-season player-season dataset covering 2008–09 through 2025–26.

What on/off expected goals mean

xGF means expected goals for; xGA means expected goals against. These are shot-quality estimates for the team, not goals credited to an individual player.

On-ice numbers cover the team’s expected goals while the player is on the ice. Off-ice numbers cover the team’s expected goals while that player is on the bench. A positive xG differential means xGF is higher than xGA.

Per-60 rates divide the expected-goal totals by the player’s on-ice or off-ice minutes and scale the result to 60 minutes. They help compare seasons with different amounts of playing time, but do not show that a player caused the team’s results.

Data sources

Roster, bios, and headshots come from the NHL and Pittsburgh Penguins. Standard player statistics come from the NHL. On/off expected-goal data comes from MoneyPuck.
