About this dashboard

This dashboard focuses on Pittsburgh Penguins regular-season data from 2008–09 through 2025–26.

On/off expected goals

Each table row represents one Penguin in one season. “On ice” is expected goals for and against while that player was on the ice. “Off ice” is the team's expected goals for and against while that player was on the bench.

The totals are MoneyPuck's xGoals values. Per-60 rates divide each total by the relevant ice or bench minutes and multiply by 60. The season chart includes players with at least 10 games and weights rates by minutes. It describes what happened during those situations; it does not establish that an individual player caused the difference.

The dataset was prepared from MoneyPuck's public regular-season skater CSVs for season years 2008 through 2025. It keeps Pittsburgh's “all situations” row for each player and selects the on-ice and off-ice xGoals and time columns. Using “all situations” avoids counting the same player multiple times. The prepared dataset has 556 player-season rows.

Source: MoneyPuck data downloads. MoneyPuck requests clear credit whenever its data is shown.
